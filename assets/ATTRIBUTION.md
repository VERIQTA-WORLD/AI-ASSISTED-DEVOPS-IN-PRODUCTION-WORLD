# Asset attribution

Project and vendor marks are preserved from Devicon, Simple Icons, CNCF artwork, and official repositories. Download records are included in docs/DEVOPS-ASSET-SOURCES.json and docs/KUBERNETES-ASSET-SOURCES.json. Marks belong to their owners and do not imply endorsement. Covers, collection cards, badges, and diagrams are original repo-native vector layouts. Concept cards are not official resource logos. AI-provider logos are absent, rather than substituted or invented. The official VERIQTA symbol was unavailable; assets use plain VERIQTA text.

Additional Devicon marks: PostgreSQL and Redis, from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/devicons/devicon@latest/icons/postgresql/postgresql-original.svg and https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/devicons/devicon@latest/icons/redis/redis-original.svg.
