# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AI-Assisted DevOps in Production World welcomes learners and contributors of all experience levels and backgrounds.

Everyone participating in this community must help maintain a respectful environment where people can ask questions, discuss engineering decisions, report mistakes, and learn without harassment.

This commitment applies regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, race, religion, sexual orientation, appearance, or professional experience.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and consideration.
- Critique ideas, code, and technical claims without attacking the person.
- Ask questions and offer feedback in good faith.
- Support beginners without ridicule or condescension.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct misleading information.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, attribution, and the repository license.
- Follow maintainer instructions when a discussion needs to pause or change direction.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not permitted:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or stalking.
- Personal insults, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption.
- Sexual harassment, unwelcome sexual attention, or sexualized content.
- Publishing another person’s private information without permission.
- Impersonation or misleading claims of affiliation.
- Spam, scams, deceptive promotion, or malicious links.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation.
- Continuing unwanted contact after being asked to stop.

## Responsible engineering participation

Technical contributions must respect the people and systems involved.

Do not:

- Share credentials, private keys, access tokens, customer data, or confidential infrastructure details.
- Upload private organizational evidence to an AI service without authorization.
- Present AI-generated recommendations as verified facts without supporting evidence.
- Fabricate test results, incident evidence, citations, or implementation outcomes.
- Encourage unauthorized access, exploitation, or changes to systems.
- Treat participation in this repository as permission to operate another person’s infrastructure.

Use authorized lab environments for exercises. Redact evidence before sharing it, and state the limits of any testing or verification you performed.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other community spaces managed for this project.

It also applies when someone officially represents the project in an external community space.

## Reporting concerns

Report misconduct privately to a repository maintainer through a private contact method listed on their public GitHub profile.

Do not post sensitive evidence or identifying details in a public issue. If no private reporting method is available, ask a maintainer to provide one without describing the incident publicly.

Include only the information needed to assess the concern:

- What happened.
- Where and approximately when it happened.
- Relevant links or redacted evidence.
- Whether the behavior is ongoing.
- Any immediate concern about further contact or retaliation.

Do not report passwords, access tokens, or private keys as evidence.

Technical vulnerabilities should follow the reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## How reports are handled

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, limit disclosure to what is reasonably needed, and consider the available evidence.

They may request clarification, consult another maintainer, or take temporary action while reviewing a concern. A maintainer directly involved in a report should not make the enforcement decision alone.

Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in every circumstance. Reporters should avoid sharing unnecessary personal information.

## Enforcement

Depending on the behavior, severity, and pattern, maintainers may:

- Request that the behavior stop.
- Remove or edit inappropriate content.
- Issue a private or public warning.
- Restrict participation temporarily.
- Permanently exclude a participant from project-managed spaces.

Serious misconduct may result in immediate restrictions without a prior warning.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and applied consistently. Technical disagreement alone is not misconduct.

## Requesting reconsideration

A participant may privately request reconsideration by identifying the decision and providing relevant new information or explaining a factual error.

Repeated contact, threats, or harassment will not be treated as an appeal.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers must model respectful behavior, address misconduct within the spaces they manage, and avoid using enforcement to silence good-faith criticism.

This Code of Conduct applies to maintainers as well as other participants.
